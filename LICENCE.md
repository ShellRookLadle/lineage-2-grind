## 🧩 About

**Lineage 2 Grind Assistant** is a comprehensive helper for the Lineage 2 PC client featuring auto-farm routes, XP rate monitor, drop tracker, inventory manager, party sync, and grind session stats.

Based on popular community tools like **L2Walker**, **Adrenaline**, and **L2Toolbox**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Grind Tools
- Auto-Farm Routes — loop recorded spawn paths for steady XP
- XP Rate Monitor — live XP/hour and time-to-level estimates
- Drop Tracker — log adena, drops, and rare item rates per mob
- Inventory Manager — auto-sort, stack, and flag full-bag events
- Party Sync — share route and buff timing with group members

### 📊 Session Analytics
- Grind Session Stats — kills, XP, adena, and drops per hour
- Leveling Planner — project level curves from current rates
- Buff Timer Overlay — track song, dance, and potion cooldowns

### 🏋️ Training & Utility
- Route Editor — build custom farm loops per zone
- Target Priority — filter mobs by name, level, or aggro type
- Safe Mode — restrict risky automation during testing
- Hotkey Profiles — swap presets for solo, party, or raid play
